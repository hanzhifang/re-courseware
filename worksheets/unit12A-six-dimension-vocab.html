Part 2 · Sound, Syllables & Word Parts

A. Read each word aloud. Then mark the syllables and stress.

Use / to divide syllables. Circle or underline the stressed syllable. If the word has a prefix, suffix, or useful word part, write its meaning. If it does not, write one pronunciation or spelling rule you notice.

Word Syllables + stress Sound / spelling rule Word part meaning
act final sound group: -ct no clear prefix/suffix
daily ai sounds like /ay/ -ly = related to time or manner
factory c sounds like /k/ before t factory = a place where things are made
operate final -ate often marks a verb oper- / operate = work, run, control
rough ough sounds like /uff/ no clear prefix/suffix
sign g is silent signal / signature are related words
simple -le can make a final syllable simple / simply are related words
uncomfortable long word: slow down and chunk it un- = not; comfort = feel good; -able = can be

Teacher Notes / Answer Key

Core meanings: act 表演/行动; daily 每日的; factory 工厂; operate 操作/运行; rough 粗糙的/不平的; sign 标志/信号; simple 简单的; uncomfortable 不舒服的。

Context answers: 1 act, 2 factory, 3 simple, 4 operate, 5 rough, 6 sign.

Part 2 suggested stress: act; DAI-ly; FAC-to-ry; OP-er-ate; rough; sign; SIM-ple; un-COM-for-ta-ble. Useful rules: rough has /uff/; sign has silent g; uncomfortable = un- + comfort + -able.
